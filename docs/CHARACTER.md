# Characters

## Structure
[LOCKED] Rarities N/R/SR/SSR. Elements 火/水/草/無. Maximum Lv30. No HP.
No active skills; characters have automatically triggered passives only.

Differentiation comes from:
1. base stat distribution
2. unique passive
3. なじみ effects

Suggested identities: pure DPS, speed, crit, reaction/application specialist, element specialist, support.

Rarity philosophy:
- N simple/stable
- R strong narrow specialization
- SR stronger conditional effects
- SSR high base stats, powerful/compound passives and may be extremely strong

Target intuition: R 3凸 ≈ SR 1凸 < SSR 0凸 overall, while lower rarity may beat SSR in a deliberately narrow niche.
